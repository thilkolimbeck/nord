02 how it works

The converter runs locally, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: the page reads your file, converts it and hands the result back as a download.

Both panels convert. Panel A becomes layers A and panel B becomes layers B, in scene I. Values go through the Stage 4's own tables, and a donor template fills in what the Stage 3 file cannot say.

03 limits

Not everything has a place on the Stage 4. Synth presets are not part of the Stage 4 library; pianos and samples carry over by their id when the Stage 4 has them; most morphs and oscillator configurations without a Stage 4 counterpart are not converted. The print-out lists every setting that is lost or approximated.

The limitation tables in the README have the full list.
